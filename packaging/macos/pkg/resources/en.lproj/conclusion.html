Strata Graphics is installed and running

The Strata Graphics icon is now in the menu bar, at the top right of the screen. It shows the server status and opens:

  • the Designer;
  • the Operator;
  • the Settings: port, video format, ST 2110 and SDI outputs.

To uninstall, open Applications ▸ Strata Graphics ▸ Uninstall Strata Graphics.
Server log: /Library/Logs/PulseCG/cg-server.log
