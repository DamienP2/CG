Welcome to Strata Graphics

This installs the Strata Graphics broadcast graphics server:

  • the server, running as a system service from startup;
  • the Strata Graphics menu bar app to control and configure it;
  • the Uninstall Strata Graphics app.

Everything goes into the Applications/Strata Graphics folder. Your data (templates, rundown, media) lives in /Library/Application Support/PulseCG and is kept on upgrade.
